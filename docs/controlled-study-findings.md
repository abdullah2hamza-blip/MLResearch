# Controlled study findings

October 22, 2026. The frozen [70-case study](results/controlled-study-v1/results.md) completed all 14 scenarios, five independent worlds per scenario, five acquisition seeds, and 14 policies/ablations. Every prediction preceded assessment and oracle access. The complete result bundle retains both assessment and known-truth metrics, source snapshots, case/member hashes, ledgers, permutations, strata, support/coverage, deferral and allocation diagnostics. Figures were visually inspected. Acquisition sources remained fixed throughout execution.

The proposed fixed-kernel discrepancy extension is **not consistently better** than classical worst-query design. With the same paid ridge-CV predictor, its mean true error area is 1.41% worse in the base linear setting and 5.08% better under nonlinear mechanism shift. Across the full matrix it has higher mean error in ten scenarios and lower error in four. The largest gain is below the dossier's 10% planning target, which was never a requirement for a positive result. Paired Monte Carlo standard errors use five independent generative worlds; acquisition seeds, queries and genes do not inflate that count. No multiple-comparison superiority claim is made.

Other comparators matter: D-optimal and disagreement designs often outperform both the extension and its primary comparator. The worst-query design targets maximum variance while the primary metric averages errors. A mean-query classical variance comparator would make that objective alignment more explicit in subsequent development; the original predeclared comparison is preserved. No new interpretation of standard optimal or Gaussian-process design as novel is warranted.

The source-uncertainty stress test exposes an important limit. At budget 192, the noiseless missing-direction case correctly identifies no supported queries. Adding source measurement noise makes the estimated feature matrix appear supported for all 150 query/seed records, while oracle latent support remains zero. Thus exact numerical linear algebra conditional on estimated features does not establish population identification. Heuristic source-stability and variance-based deferral do not certify safety under systematic source error. For `worst_query` with fixed ridge at budget 192, Gaussian coverage conditional on finite widths drops from about 95.4% in the base setting to 86.4% under nonlinear shift and 78.1% with persistent source-feature error SD 1. These are qualified diagnostics, not validated adaptive intervals.

Allocation behavior helps interpret the extension. In the base case at budget 192, it opens about 10.12 interventions and buys 45.44 controls on average, versus 8.64 and 51.04 for classical worst-query. Their fixed design rules do not estimate the realized nonlinear bias from paid outcome residuals; the retained mechanism-allocation check compares their action sequences across matched worlds. A gain under shift therefore does not demonstrate successful online detection of that shift. Gene-coordinate and source-identity permutations can eliminate source utility and make extra acquisition harmful. All such failures remain in the tables.

The original weight `.25` was fixed for this simulation matrix. The separate [Frangieh tuning](development-tuning-findings.md) selected weight `1` and a fixed grid, but nearly all its predictions approach zero and its tiny policy differences do not establish a practical benefit. Keep those study stages separate. The simulation's fixed meta-development charge remains 4,960 units; future external configurations must use the updated 6,344-unit development record.

This milestone validates the simulation machinery and records a mixed, mainly negative acquisition result. Synthetic Gaussian worlds do not validate regulatory transport or biological generalization. Prior-method reconstruction, stronger source uncertainty and empirical common-control analysis still need package integration and checks, followed by a frozen external evaluation in both valid directions. The full implementation goal remains active.
