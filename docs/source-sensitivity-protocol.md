# Source-feature sensitivity protocol

October 22, 2026, before external recipient scoring. This is a diagnostic of source measurement error conditional on the fixed paid acquisition ledger. It does not supply a joint confidence interval or validate a deferral guarantee.

The episode stores each intervention's first-source-half minus second-source-half contrast, with the same source-control half contrast subtracted from every intervention. Construct two joint perturbations `source_effect +/- half_difference/2` and refit the source PCA independently in each. For even treatment and control counts these reproduce the corresponding half-sample effects up to stored floating-point precision. For odd counts they are symmetric perturbations around the full mean, not literal half means. Record the number of odd-count source conditions, including controls. Retain the dependence induced by shared source controls; do not perturb each intervention's control independently.

For each paid checkpoint, refit the fixed-ridge and paid-validation `ridge_cv` predictors using the same purchased recipient statistics and the same penalty grid. Recompute unregularized support in each perturbed coordinate system. Retain per-query maximum squared prediction displacement from the original, between-half squared displacement, both-half support, support disagreement, and selected half-specific penalties. These fields require no recipient observations beyond the original ledger. The acquisition policy is not rerun, so the diagnostic excludes source-error effects on acquisition. The neural reconstruction is not refitted in this diagnostic. Neither the predictions nor the primary endpoint are replaced by half-sample estimates.

Under independent equal-size IID cells, a half-difference divided by two has the variance of the corresponding full-sample mean error, including the common-control covariance across interventions. A repeated-experiment Monte Carlo test checks that scale and covariance. One realized split is insufficient to estimate an error distribution. Biological shifts, omitted mechanisms, unequal cells, guide/batch dependence and population support errors remain outside this diagnostic. Two noisy halves can agree on incorrect support; the completed controlled study demonstrates why numerical support cannot certify population identification.

The existing source signal-to-estimated-noise deferral flag remains a separate heuristic. Report its retained-risk curve and observed failures without calling either that flag or half agreement a calibrated accept/reject rule. Scorer-side query and cell uncertainty remain separate as specified in [the assessment protocol](assessment-uncertainty-protocol.md).
