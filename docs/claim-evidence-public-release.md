# Claim-to-evidence record for the public research release

Prepared October 28, 2026 by AI-assisted internal review. This record separates evidence authentication, re-analysis of saved outputs and full experimental reproduction. It is not independent human peer review, biological validation or venue acceptance. Use the versioned release manifest for the hosting commit/tag; the numerical studies retain their own scientific source identities below.

**R** means reproduced during this public-release task from saved outputs. **P** means supported by a traceable prior run or review whose retained evidence was inspected/authenticated. **U** means unverified or outside the supported claim. An R calculation does not change its underlying experiment from P into a newly executed experiment. Conditional resampling ranges are P here unless explicitly rerun; no full acquisition study or full raw preparation was repeated for this map.

The [machine-readable record](evidence/claim-evidence-public-release.json) contains fresh calculations, tolerances, source identities and artifact hashes. It authenticated all 150 inputs and 25 outputs listed in `paper/generated/asset-manifest.json`, independently recomputed the primary areas, all 32 robustness contrasts, original/favorable-cell query influence, all 14 controlled true-error contrasts, known-truth support/coverage and direct-query endpoint summaries. No numerical discrepancy requiring a manuscript correction was found.

## Reproduction levels and remaining limits

The machine record includes independent arithmetic results and uses absolute tolerance `1e-14` for robustness mean differences, `1e-12` for relative changes and original controlled paired means; displayed manuscript values use their stated rounding. Conditional ranges, complete cell-bank generation, fit selection and historical ledger/array audits are not relabeled as freshly repeated merely because retained outputs authenticate. The historical [scientific review](release-scientific-review-v2.md) and [software review](release-software-review-v2.md) document additional prior inspection and independent algebra checks; the final release checklist records new clean-environment, PDF, notebook and hosting checks separately.

No supporting claim in this record needs invented experimental evidence. The following stronger claims remain **U** and are not asserted by the manuscript: biological transportability to a new independently replicated context; prospective laboratory or currency savings; a universally superior acquisition policy; calibrated end-to-end adaptive intervals; equivalence/no possible benefit; and independent biological replication from repeated splits, seeds, genes or directions. A demonstration or saved-output notebook is not a full-data experimental reproduction.
